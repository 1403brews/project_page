I am a robotics researcher working on UAV autonomy, SLAM, and sensor fusion — building perception, planning, and control systems that let robots navigate, explore, and make decisions in unknown and dynamic environments. My recent work spans LiDAR/visual-inertial odometry, motion planning and obstacle avoidance, and vision-language navigation for embodied AI.

I am an undergraduate at Delhi Technological University and Vice Captain & Autonomy Lead at UAS-DTU, where I led the autonomy stack for the DARPA Triage Challenge (2nd place, self-funded category, across two phases — $210,000 in prizes). I have since worked as a research intern at the Robotics Research Center, IIIT Hyderabad, and with SAIR Lab (University at Buffalo) and FPV Labs.

Nishant Chandna, et al.

CoRL 2026 — Under Review.

A 3D-grounded method that predicts pixel-level planning cost for an arbitrary set of images and subgoal pixel, coupling learned cost-map prediction with control-conditioned planning to outperform connectivity-based search planners across diverse navigation tasks.

Projects

Drone-based Forest Mapping

Deployed a PX4-based UAV in a simulated forest in Gazebo; implemented mapping with GenZ-ICP and benchmarked against KISS-ICP, then fused ICP with GPS and IMU using dynamic confidence-based weighting for robust state estimation. Designed custom environments to validate the mapping and localization pipeline.

C++ · Python · Bash · PX4 · ROS

Collision Avoidance with EGO-Planner & VINS-Mono

GPS-denied autonomous navigation and collision avoidance indoors, using VINS-Mono for visual-inertial state estimation and EGO-Planner for real-time local trajectory generation. Integrated perception, planning, and control on a lightweight quadrotor running PX4/ROS for agile, high-speed obstacle avoidance in cluttered 3D spaces.

C++ · VIO · PX4 · ROS

Reward-Function Improvement in PyFlyt (RL for UAVs)

Enhanced the reward function of an open RL environment by introducing a penalty on the rate of change of orientation across previous time steps, improving drone stability in tasks such as hovering and pole balancing and accelerating convergence.

Python · Reinforcement Learning · Stable-Baselines3 · Gymnasium

UAV Fruit Detection & Counting — ICUAS’24

Autonomous greenhouse navigation and fruit counting combining YOLOv8 detection, LiDAR-camera 3D mapping, and TSP-A* path planning. Achieved 98% accuracy in simulation and 90% in real-world testing.

Python · ROS · YOLOv8 · Gazebo

DARPA Triage Challenge — Autonomy Stack

End-to-end autonomy for coordinated multi-robot triage across ground and aerial platforms, enabling navigation, exploration, and decision-making in unknown, dynamic environments. 2nd place (self-funded category) across Phase-1 and Phase-2.
