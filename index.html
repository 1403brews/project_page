Nishant Chandna

Robotics Researcher · SLAM, Egocentric Perception & Embodied AI

I am a robotics researcher working on SLAM, egocentric perception, and sim-to-real manipulation — building perception, planning, and evaluation systems that let robots navigate, explore, and act in unknown and dynamic environments. I am an undergraduate at Delhi Technological University and Vice Captain & Autonomy Lead at UAS-DTU.

I led the autonomy stack for the DARPA Triage Challenge (2nd place, self-funded category, across two phases — $210,000 in prizes). Since then I have worked as a research intern at the Robotics Research Center, IIIT Hyderabad, and currently with FPV Labs on egocentric pose estimation and sim-to-real manipulation, and with SAIR Lab (University at Buffalo) on vision-language navigation.

A theme runs through most of it: I care about whether a number means what it claims to mean — validating commodity pose against motion capture, holding the confounds fixed when two policy checkpoints are compared, and reporting the error a reference introduces rather than hiding it.

DAMM-LOAM — Degeneracy-Aware LiDAR Odometry

Improves LiDAR SLAM in degenerate environments such as corridors and tunnels, using normal-image feature classification and degeneracy-aware adaptive weighting, plus Scan-Context loop closure and factor-graph optimization for global consistency.

FairEval SO-101 — Honest Policy Evaluation

An evaluation harness that makes two real-robot policy checkpoints actually comparable: every checkpoint faces the same counterbalanced object layouts in the same order, restaged against the reference frames the first run captured. Paired with mechanism-scoped perturbations — defocus, illumination, framing — held at fixed severity, turning a score into a curve.

LeRobotPyTorchOpenCVNext.jsSO-101

Drone-based Forest Mapping

Deployed a PX4 UAV in a simulated forest in Gazebo; implemented mapping with GenZ-ICP and benchmarked against KISS-ICP, then fused ICP with GPS and IMU using dynamic confidence-based weighting for robust state estimation.

C++PythonBashPX4ROS

Collision Avoidance with EGO-Planner & VINS-Mono

GPS-denied autonomous navigation and collision avoidance indoors, using VINS-Mono for visual-inertial state estimation and EGO-Planner for real-time local trajectory generation on a lightweight quadrotor — agile, high-speed obstacle avoidance in cluttered 3D spaces.
