Nishant Chandna

Robotics Researcher · UAV Autonomy, SLAM & Sensor Fusion

I am a robotics researcher working on UAV autonomy, SLAM, and sensor fusion — building perception, planning, and control systems that let robots navigate, explore, and make decisions in unknown and dynamic environments. I am an undergraduate at Delhi Technological University and Vice Captain & Autonomy Lead at UAS-DTU.

I led the autonomy stack for the DARPA Triage Challenge (2nd place, self-funded category, across two phases — $210,000 in prizes). Since then I have worked as a research intern at the Robotics Research Center, IIIT Hyderabad, and with SAIR Lab (University at Buffalo) and FPV Labs, on topics spanning LiDAR/visual-inertial odometry, motion planning and obstacle avoidance, and vision-language navigation for embodied AI.
